Process
How Jan Sunwai Works
1
📝
Submit Grievance
Fill the online form with your complaint details, department, and supporting documents or photos.
›
2
✅
Acknowledgement
Receive a unique grievance ID via SMS and email within 24 hours for tracking your complaint.
›
3
🏛️
Department Review
The concerned department reviews your grievance, assigns an officer, and prepares a response.
›
4
🎙️
Hearing Scheduled
Attend a virtual or in-person Jan Sunwai session. Present your case directly before the presiding officer.
›
5
🎯
Resolution & Closure
Receive official resolution. Rate your satisfaction. Escalate if dissatisfied within 30 days.
