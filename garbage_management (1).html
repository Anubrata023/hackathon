Citizen's Waste-Wise Guide

Small habits that create big impact for our cities and environment

🪣
Use Separate Bins

Keep two bins at home — one green for wet waste, one blue for dry. Label them clearly.

🛍️
Refuse Single-Use Plastic

Carry cloth bags. Plastic under 75 microns is banned in Assam. Report violations at the helpline.

🌾
Compost Kitchen Waste

Convert organic waste into rich compost for your garden. Reduce 60% of household waste instantly.

⏰
Follow Collection Timing

Keep waste bags ready by 6:30 AM. Do not leave bags outside after collection hours to avoid spillage.
