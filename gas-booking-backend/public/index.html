How OTP Booking Works
Enter Mobile & Details
Enter your registered mobile number and LPG Consumer ID. An OTP is sent to your phone instantly.
Verify OTP
Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your mobile. The OTP is valid for 10 minutes and cannot be reused.
Select Distributor & Slot
Choose your nearest authorised distributor and a convenient home delivery time slot.
Confirm & Collect
Confirm your booking. Cylinder is delivered to your door. Subsidy is auto-credited to your bank account within 3 days.
